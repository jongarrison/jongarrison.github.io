This is the starting point for an independent archive of my long-running Earthwandering account that had been hosted on Flickr.

For a bunch of years, I had a camera hanging on my shoulder at all times. Sometimes I wish I still did, but the ever present phone camera seems to fulfill most of the itch, but takes lower quality photos and two hands to operate. Maybe someday I'll create a phone case that solves this problem and the flow of in the moment adventure shots will start up again.
